# The five questions

Ask them in this order, one at a time.

1. What eats most of your week that isn't the work you're paid for? Roughly how many hours a week does it take?
2. When a new enquiry comes in, what happens next, and how quickly does someone reply?
3. What do you find yourself chasing people for? Payments, paperwork, replies, reviews?
4. What do you type out again and again? Quotes, emails, reports, notes?
5. If you could wave a wand and one job in the business would just be done every week, which would it be?
